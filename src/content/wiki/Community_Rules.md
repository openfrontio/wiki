The **community rules** apply across every OpenFront space: public games, the official [Discord](/Discord) servers and the [clan](/Clan) system. Breaking them is punishable, up to a game ban. Use the **Report Player** button in the player panel (see [Accounts](/Accounts)) to report botting, teaming, bad names or griefing straight to the moderation team.

## Fair play and anti-teaming {#Fair_play_and_anti-teaming}

- **No collusion in solo FFA.** Collusion, intentional teaming or coordinated diplomacy in solo [Free-For-All](/Free_for_All) lobbies is strictly forbidden and punishable.
- **No cross-team collusion.** In [team](/Teams) modes, colluding with players on an opposing team is forbidden in the same way.
- **The "Mitochondria" (mito) playstyle.** Playing as a _mito_ — supporting another player organically from within a match you are already in — is allowed. **Pre-arranging** it, by coordinating with friends or other players before entering the lobby to act as their dedicated mito, counts as intentional collusion and is strictly punishable.

## Conduct {#Conduct}

Harassment, hate speech, toxic behaviour and unsolicited DM recruitment are prohibited in all community spaces, in game and on Discord.

## Finding a clan {#Finding_a_clan}

Looking for a clan? Browse the active recruitment threads in the official clan forums on the main Discord. To start your own, see [requesting a clan](/Clan#Requesting_a_clan).
