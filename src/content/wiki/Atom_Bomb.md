The **Atom Bomb** is the cheapest and least powerful nuclear weapon in the game. It is priced at 750,000 [Gold](/Gold).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

# Launching {#Launching}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/300px-Atom_Bomb_s_explosion.png" decoding="async" width="300" height="218" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="518" data-file-height="377"><figcaption>An Atom Bomb's explosion (in Green) with Europe for scale.</figcaption></figure>

To launch an **Atom Bomb**, first right-click onto any surface in the map. Then, open the radial menu. Then open the Build Menu at the desired location and select "Atom Bomb". Launching a Atom Bomb requires one or more [Missile Silos](/Missile_Silo) owned by the player launching it. The Bomb will then be launched from the nearest [Missile Silo](/Missile_Silo),  assuming the [Missile Silo](/Missile_Silo) is not reloading.

# Missile's behavior {#Missile's_behavior}

Once spawned from a [Missile Silo](/Missile_Silo), the missile will move first in the x direction (horizontally) towards the target location until the x location of the missile is the same as the target x location, then the missile will move in the y direction (vertically) towards the target location (there are random small x/y movements sprinkled in).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The missile moves four pixels per game tick. The missile is not influenced by below water/land. The Atom Bomb will be intercepted when in an 100 pixel radius of an active [SAM Launcher](/SAM_Launcher).

# Explosion {#Explosion}

Once the missile meets its target location, the Atom Bomb will explode, [irradiating](/Fallout) all terrain in a 15 pixel radius and about half<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> of the terrain in a 40 pixel radius. The Atom Bomb does not [irradiate](/Fallout) any water. The Atom Bomb will remove troops proportional to land percentage taken; if the nuke irradiates 10% of a player's land, 10% of their troops will be removed (killed).
