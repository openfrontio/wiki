**Central America** is a North American map in [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) covering the isthmus from the Yucatán peninsula down to Panama, with the Caribbean coast, the Mosquito Coast and the Pacific side of the region.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Central_America_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="501" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="2500" data-file-height="1957"><figcaption>The Central America map.</figcaption></figure>

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 22 named [nations](/Nations), including Yucatan, Quintana Roo, Campeche, Guatemala, Belize, El Salvador, Honduras and Nicaragua, plus a pool of 41 additional coastal nations (Mexican, Guatemalan, Honduran, Nicaraguan, Costa Rican and Panamanian cities) that the game can draw on to fill larger lobbies.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** North America, New
- **Nations:** 22 (+41 additional)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 5
