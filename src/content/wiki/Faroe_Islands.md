[Map](/Maps) of the Faroe Islands, a real life island cluster located in the north Atlantic sea, between Iceland and Great Britain. It is currently under the Kingdom of Denmark.

The [map](/Maps) itself presents a lot of waterways and it is a good [map](/Maps) for naval invasions and trading via [ports](/Port).

## Nations {#Map_nations}

The map features 6 named [nations](/Nations), including Suduroy Region, Sandoy Region, Vagar Region, Streymoy Region, Eysturoy Region and Nordoyar Region.

## Map data {#Map_data}

- **Categories:** Europe
- **Nations:** 6
- **Public rotation frequency:** 4

## Technical {#Technical}

The size of the [map](/Maps) is 1600 x 2000.
