The **controls** for OpenFront use a combination of keyboard and mouse actions. They can be rebound by going into Settings → Keybinds. As of [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0), the `F` boat-attack key, `R` and box-selecting [warships](/Warship) are also rebindable, and `F` or `Escape` cancels a warship selection.

[Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) moved several defaults: boat attack is now `B` (was `F`), `F` opens the redesigned emoji menu, `R` opens quick chat on the player under your cursor, and select-all-warships moved to `X`. `K`/`L` send or break an alliance and `N` targets a player. The arrow keys and zoom keys are now real keybinds too, so assigning them elsewhere no longer fires both actions. In the emoji menu, `Q` `W` `E` `A` `S` `D` fire your six Favorites. [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) added donate hotkeys — `J` sends troops and `O` opens the send-gold window for the ally under your cursor — and `I` to open their player panel, plus hold-`H` to build a separate structure instead of upgrading a nearby one. Pressing a build key again on the same spot steps the amount 1 → 5 → 10 → 15 (up to 50) and `Alt`+scroll adjusts it by 1. Modifier keys (`Shift`, `Ctrl`, `Alt`) can be bound, the performance overlay key (`Shift`+`D`) can be rebound, and on AZERTY and other layouts the defaults and hints now show the keys on your own keyboard (`ZQSD` instead of `WASD`).
