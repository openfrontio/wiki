[Map](/Maps) of the Falkland Islands located outside of Argentina.

It consists of two main islands, surrounded by water on all sides. There are plenty of tiny islands scattered around the main islands.

## Nations {#Map_nations}

The map features 12 named [nations](/Nations), including Albermarle, Weddell, Fox Bay, East Falkland, Saunders and Dunbar and South Lefonia.

## Map data {#Map_data}

- **Categories:** South America
- **Nations:** 12
- **Public rotation frequency:** 4

## Technical {#Technical}

The size of the [map](/Maps) is 2100 x 1400.
