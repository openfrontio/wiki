**Ottoman Empire** is a historical map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0), credited in the release notes to **halil1054** and **RickD004** — the empire in 1683, at its greatest extent in Europe, stretching from Hungary and the Crimea to Egypt, the Levant and Mesopotamia. There is no Suez Canal, so the Mediterranean and the Red Sea are not connected.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Ottoman_Empire_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="485" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="2816" data-file-height="2132"><figcaption>The Ottoman Empire map.</figcaption></figure>

The map spans three continents and is one of the few in the game categorised as European, Asian and African at once. The Mediterranean, Black Sea and Red Sea coasts suit [ports](/Port) and [warships](/Warship), while the Balkans and Anatolia are mountainous and slow to expand through.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 46 named [nations](/Nations) based on the empire's eyalets, sanjaks and vassal states, including Istanbul, Rumeli, Anadolu, Budin, Egri, Temesvar, Bosna, Silistre, Kamaniçe, Kirim Hanligi (the Crimean Khanate), Kudüs, Özi and Adana.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Europe, Asia, Africa
- **Nations:** 46
- **Public rotation frequency:** not set in the map data
