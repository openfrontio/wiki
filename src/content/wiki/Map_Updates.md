Maps can change between game versions. When a new update comes out it can add new [maps](/Maps), redraw or rebalance existing ones, or change how often a map turns up in public games — so a map can look different from when you last played it.

## New maps {#New_maps}

An update can add new maps to the game. Recently added maps are tagged **New** in the map picker for one major version, then move into their regular category.

- [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0): [Paris](/Paris), [London](/London), [Mexico](/Mexico), [Canada](/Canada), [Türkiye](/Turkiye), [Ottoman Empire](/Ottoman_Empire), [Zealandia](/Zealandia) and [Brahmaputra](/Brahmaputra), plus [World 10000](/World_10000) and [World 4000](/World_4000) (which replaces the retired [Giant World Map](/Giant_World_Map)). [World](/World) was rebuilt and [Africa](/Africa) got new, greener terrain.
- [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0): [Madagascar](/Madagascar), [Horn of Africa](/Horn_of_Africa), [Vancouver Island](/Vancouver_Island), [Cape of Good Hope](/Cape_of_Good_Hope), [Canary Islands](/Canary_Islands), [Bab el-Mandeb Strait](/Bab_el-Mandeb_Strait), [Rio de Janeiro](/Rio_de_Janeiro), [Pulicat Lake](/Pulicat_Lake), [Benelux](/Benelux), [New Zealand](/New_Zealand) and [Brain Rot](/Brain_Rot), plus the Halloween-only [Ommatrypta](/Ommatrypta).
- [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0): [Yangtze River](/Yangtze_River), [Cape Cod](/Cape_Cod), [Channel Islands](/Channel_Islands) and [Gulf of Mexico](/Gulf_of_Mexico). [China](/China) was redrawn with modern borders and the classic map kept as [Qing China](/Qing_China).
- [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0): 22 new maps, including [Sol](/Sol), [Russia](/Russia), [United States](/United_States), [France](/France), [Germany](/Germany), [Vietnam](/Vietnam) and [Scandinavia](/Scandinavia).

## Update 36.0 map changes {#Update_36.0_map_changes}

- [World](/World) and [World 4000](/World_4000) were rebuilt with new terrain, rivers and softer decoration layers. World 4000 replaces [Giant World Map](/Giant_World_Map), which is retired. [World 10000](/World_10000) is a new 10,000-pixel-wide map for custom games and tournaments.
- New, greener terrain for [Africa](/Africa), with new nations and fixed flags.
- [Los Angeles](/Los_Angeles) got a rebuilt road network and new nations; [Las Vegas Strip](/Las_Vegas_Strip) lost most northern blocks and gained crosswalks; Henichesk Strait was added to [Crimea](/Crimea); [Korea](/Korea) is bigger; [Channel Islands](/Channel_Islands) was reworked; fixes for [China](/China) and [Qing China](/Qing_China).
- [Brain Rot](/Brain_Rot) got new, calmer decoration art now that Halloween is over.
- The map picker gained an **Atlas** tab (a world map with a pin per real-world map), and every map card now shows its designer, description, size, land share, nation count and expected players.
- Map descriptions were written for many maps, and the v35 maps are no longer marked New.
- Boats no longer fail at, or detour around, river bends that double back ([Didier](/Didier), [Conakry](/Conakry), [Russia](/Russia), [China](/China), [Europe](/Europe)).
- Flags for all 12 Dutch provinces were added.

## Update 35.0 map changes {#Update_35.0_map_changes}

- Public lobbies are no longer capped at 125 players: lobby size now comes from the map's land, so [The Box](/The_Box) fits 210, [Alps](/Alps) 185 and [Middle East](/Middle_East) 170. Alps and The Box appear more often.
- The v34 maps left the **New** category, and [Channel Islands](/Channel_Islands)' FFA rotation was lowered.
- [Brain Rot](/Brain_Rot) and [Ommatrypta](/Ommatrypta) are boosted in public rotation for Halloween.
- Better terrain for [Pulicat Lake](/Pulicat_Lake).
- v0.35.1 added Antarctic [tribe](/Tribes) themes on [Antarctica](/Antarctica) and [Deglaciated Antarctica](/Deglaciated_Antarctica).
- Map layers now ship as lossy WebP, about 9× smaller.

## Update 34.0 map changes {#Update_34.0_map_changes}

- [Milky Way](/MilkyWay) map update; small modifications to [Sol](/Sol) and [Archipelago Sea](/ArchipelagoSea).
- Connected the Rhine to the sea on [Germany](/Germany), which now appears in the Countries section.
- Fixed the US states of Minnesota and Georgia on [United States](/United_States).
- Fixed river pixel errors on [Hecate Strait](/Hecate_Strait) and [Baltics](/Baltics), and typos in Baltics nation names.
- [Labyrinth](/Labyrinth)'s public rotation frequency was reduced from 6 to 2, and [Luna](/Luna) and [Chopping Block](/Chopping_Block) were removed from public rotation.
- Public rotation now supports per-mode map weights and per-map modifier overrides, and per-map team frequencies were restored.
