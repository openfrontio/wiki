[Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) rebuilt this map (by **Patrick Plays Badly**) with new terrain, rivers and softer decoration layers. It stays the smaller world map, with almost no rivers: just the continents, oceans and a few major waterways. For the bigger versions see [World 4000](/World_4000) and [World 10000](/World_10000).

Map of the World (sometimes referred to as the globe or Earth map) is a map that shows all of the 7 major continents of the world as well as all 5 major oceans of the world. This also included islands such as the Caribbean islands, the British isles, Iceland, New Zealand, Madagascar, Japan and others. However, smaller islands are not shown due to the size of the map.

The map's [terrain](/Terrain) is comprised of an even amount of flat, hilly and mountainous [terrain](/Terrain) that is quite evenly spread through the [map](/Maps). However, Asia tends to be more mountainous, Africa tends to be more hilly and South America tends to be more flat.

## Nations {#Map_nations}

The map features 70 named [nations](/Nations), including Alaska, California, Canada, Cuba, Greenland, Hawaii, Mexico, Nunavut, Quebec and Texas.
