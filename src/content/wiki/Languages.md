The game currently supports these languages (Vietnamese was added in [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0)):

- Arabic
- Bengali
- Brazilian Portuguese
- Bulgarian
- Catalan
- Czech
- Danish
- Dutch
- English
- Esperanto
- Estonian
- European Portuguese
- Finnish
- French
- Galician
- German
- Greek
- Hebrew
- Hindi
- Hungarian
- Indonesian
- Italian
- Japanese
- Korean
- Macedonian
- Persian
- Polish
- Russian
- Serbo-Croatian
- Simplified Chinese
- Slovak
- Slovenian
- Spanish
- Swedish
- Swiss German
- Toki Pona
- Traditional Chinese
- Turkish
- Ukrainian
- Vietnamese

On the [Steam](/Steam) client, Steam's locale is used as the default language.
