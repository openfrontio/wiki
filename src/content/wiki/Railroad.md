When a [factory](/Factory) and a [city](/City) or [port](/Port) are placed near eachother,  railroads will be spawned to connect the [buildings](/Buildings) together. [Trains](/Train) may only travel where a Railroad exists.

Adding further [buildings](/Buildings) within this distance will cause more rail to be spawned, creating a mesh. It is also possible to make a [train](/Train) loop.

On launch, the following [buildings](/Buildings) are able to be connected:

- [Factory](/Factory)
- [City](/City)
- [Port](/Port)

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/200px-Factory_port_trains.png" decoding="async" width="200" height="149" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="457" data-file-height="340"><figcaption><a href="/Factory" class="" title="Factories">Factories</a> and <a href="/Port" title="Port">ports</a> connected by <span class="wiki-deadlink">Railroads</span> with a <a href="/Train" title="Train">Train</a> on the tracks</figcaption></figure>

The maximum distance within which a [factory](/Factory), [city](/City) or [port](/Port) can connect to another station to form a railroad is 80.

The maximum length that a single railroad connection can be is 100.

As of [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0), rails can no longer cross impassable [terrain](/Terrain), and trains no longer despawn when a track splits. Railroads and trains can also be recoloured with cosmetic effects from the store.
