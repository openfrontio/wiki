**London** is a European city map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) — the Greater London area, borough by borough, with the Thames winding through the middle. The map was designed by **Tristar** and added to the game by **RickD004**.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/London_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="544" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="2000" data-file-height="1700"><figcaption>The London map.</figcaption></figure>

Founded by the Romans in AD 43 as Londinium, London is the capital of England and the United Kingdom and its largest metropolis, with a history spanning nearly 2,000 years.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 32 named [nations](/Nations) based on the London boroughs, including Bromley, Bexley, Havering, Barking and Dagenham, Greenwich, Lewisham, Croydon and Sutton. A further 30 names (such as Ruislip, Harefield, Pinner, Stanmore and Edgware) are held in reserve and used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Europe, New
- **Nations:** 32 (+30 reserve names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** not set in the map data
