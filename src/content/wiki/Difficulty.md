Difficulty levels in [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) change the intensity of the game for private and [singleplayer](/Single_Player) lobbies.

When creating a new lobby, several options (such as changing [maps](/Maps) and [Game Conditions](/Game_Settings)) can be selected by the host. Beneath the [map](/Maps) choice is the choice of difficulty. The difficulty ranges between four levels:

- Relaxed (Easiest Difficulty)
- Balanced (Default Difficulty)
- Intense

- Impossible (Hardest Difficulty)

These difficulties do not change how [nations](/Nations) act and play the game, they instead change how they get resources directly.

## Trivia {#Trivia}

- While there is a defined "Difficulty Modifier" function with large gaps between difficulty values (1, 3, 9, 18), this value is actually not used anywhere within the source code.
