**Communication** in [Openfront](/OpenFront.io) allows players and [nations](/Nations) to send each other emojis, predefined chat messages, and attack intents in order to plan for attack, defense, and ultimately the win. Players who prefer not to see emojis can turn them off with the **Disable emojis** setting, which suppresses all emoji display.

## Sending Emojis {#Sending_Emojis}

Players can send emojis to other humans or nations. Emojis sent between humans has no effect on the game itself. Sending certain emojis to a nation can affect the relationship score with them:
