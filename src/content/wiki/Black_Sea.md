[Map](/Maps) of areas surrounding the Black Sea. Mountains everywhere with most of the plains in the northwest and southeast. Centre of the [map](/Maps) is dominated by water and there are several rivers going across the [map](/Maps). It has the island of Crimea, but most of the map is land. So, it is somewhat harder to attack in the map outside of Crimea. Most of the [map](/Maps) is mountain, and the flattest part is Georgia. (Not to be confused with the state)
