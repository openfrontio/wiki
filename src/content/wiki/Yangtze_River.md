**Yangtze River** is an Asian river map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0) by **crunchybbb** — an upside-down-U-shaped pipe river map following the lower Yangtze from Nanjing to the Shanghai delta.
