Trading serves as a major source of [gold](/Gold) income and strategic depth, encouraging players to balance expansion, [alliances](/Ally), and defense to optimize their economic networks.

Trading in [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) occurs exclusively between buildings of the same type, [ports](/Port) can only trade with other [ports](/Port), and [factories](/Factory) only with other [factories](/Factory).

This allows players to establish internal trade routes between their own structures if they own multiple [Ports](/Port) or [Factories](/Factory). [Trading](/Trade) between different players, often yielding greater rewards due to increased distance between trade endpoints.

Trade Units are generated automatically from [ports](/Port) and [factories](/Factory) over time, with the spawn rate increasing based on the total number of that building type owned by all players. However, the benefit of additional [buildings](/Buildings) diminishes due to scaling limitations. When a trade Unit completes its route, both the source and destination owners receive [gold](/Gold).

Longer routes grant higher payouts due to distance-based scaling.

The selection of trade destinations favors nearby or [allied](/Ally) [buildings](/Buildings), making diplomacy and geography important factors in maximizing trade efficiency. While trade Units are protected when near the shoreline or their destination, they remain vulnerable while in transit. Enemy [warships](/Warship) can intercept and capture [trade ships](/Trade_Ship), redirecting the [trade](/Trade_Ship) or eliminating it entirely if no valid destination exists.
