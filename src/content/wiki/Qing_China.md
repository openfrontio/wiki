**Qing China** is an Asian country map in [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) by **crunchybbb**. It is the original Qing-dynasty-themed [China](/China) map added in [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0); when [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0) redrew China with modern borders, the classic map was kept under this new name.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 32 named [nations](/Nations), including British Hong Kong, Guangdong, Tannu Tuva, Boxer Rebellion, Taiping Heavenly Kingdom and Sichuan.

- **Categories:** Asia, Countries
- **Nations:** 32
- **Public rotation frequency:** 4
