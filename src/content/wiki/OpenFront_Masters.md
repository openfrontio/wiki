OpenFront Masters (OFM) was announced 07/07 2025 to be the official Tournament team.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

To be able to participate and interact you have to use [discord](/Discord), you can find the link in the official [OpenFront.io](/OpenFront.io) [discord](/Discord).

OpenFront Masters intended use is to host events and competitions with a public leaderboard.

Outside of tournaments, there was a mention that it would be a place to support future beta testing and run community play tests.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>

## Rules and regulatory {#Rules_and_regulatory}

In OpenFront Masters they use referees who are not allowed to participate in matches to stay impartial.

There are rules set up to prevent cheating, interfering and match fixing. To mention a few.
