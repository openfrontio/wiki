**Brahmaputra** is an Asian map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0), credited in the release notes to **Avner** and **RickD004** (the map data lists **novc** as the designer) — the Assam valley, split in two by the Brahmaputra river, with Majuli and the sandbar islands in between.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Brahmaputra_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="449" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="1460" data-file-height="1024"><figcaption>The Brahmaputra map.</figcaption></figure>

It is built as a **two-[team](/Teams) map**: north bank against south bank, with each team spawning along its own side of the river. You can cross the river by boat almost anywhere, so the whole bank is the front line.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 37 named [nations](/Nations) based on towns of Assam and its neighbours, including Barpeta, Nalbari, Mangaldoi, Tezpur, Biswanath, Gohpur, Itanagar, North Lakhimpur, Dhemaji, Silapathar, Haflong, Nongstoin and Trongsa. A further 30 names are held in reserve and used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Asia, New
- **Nations:** 37 (+30 reserve names)
- **Teams:** designed for 2 teams (north bank vs south bank)
- **Public rotation frequency:** not set in the map data
