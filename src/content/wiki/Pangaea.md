[Map](/Maps) of the old continent Pangaea, which already broke apart in real life. It has a few rivers here and there.

This [map](/Maps) is optimal for land attacks, as there is only one continent, and it is easy to attack other parts of it.

## Nations {#Map_nations}

The map features 29 named [nations](/Nations), including South Africa, Madagascar, Egypt, United States, Canada and Mexico.
