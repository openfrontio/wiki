Update 20.0, previously named update 0.20.0, got deployed in 2025 on March 19/20th (depends on your timezone), adding various new features.

## New Features {#New_Features}

### Maps & Territories {#Maps_&_Territories}

- North America map got lakes, rivers, more [bots](/Bots), and a larger size.
- Africa map got lakes, rivers, and more [bots](/Bots).
- Gateway to the Atlantic map added
- All 50 US State flags can be picked as player flags
- 24 British and Irish Nation flags

### Combat & Military {#Combat_&_Military}

- [Warships](/Warship) don't heal or capture [trade ships](/Trade_Ship) when their owning player has no [port](/Port).
- All units have a slightly faster attack speed
- Medium and big players have a higher attack speed, and the opposite for huge players.
- [SAM](/SAM_Launcher) sites now have faster missiles, circular range instead of square, and a slightly faster attack rate.
- [SAM](/SAM_Launcher) sites also have a 75% interception likelihood for [nukes](/Atom_Bomb) and [hydrogen bombs](/Hydrogen_Bomb) and turn red while reloading

## UI & Graphics {#UI_&_Graphics}

- When hovering over a country, you get detailed information towards the top right of your screen, which includes number of attacking troops, [cities](/City), and [missile launchers](/Missile_Silo).
- [SAM](/SAM_Launcher) Sites now appear thicker on the map with slightly different border colors.
- Colorized map thumbnails are back
- Fixed water depth/darkness gradient from coastlines.

## Stability & Fixes {#Stability_&_Fixes}

- Identified and fixed issue with lobbies not starting when max players exceeded.

## Miscellaneous {#Miscellaneous}

- Integrated colorized thumbnail creation to map creation scripts
