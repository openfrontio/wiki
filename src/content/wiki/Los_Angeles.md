**Los Angeles** is a North American city map in [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) by **Patrick Plays Badly** — "What's LA without its roads? No, really??" The freeways and roads are drawn as water. [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) rebuilt the road network and added new nations.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 39 named [nations](/Nations), including Santa Clarita, Glendale, Pasadena, Anaheim, Walnut, Santa Ana, Huntington Beach, Long Beach, Torrance and Thousand Oaks, plus 146 custom tribe names for when a game needs more.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** North America
- **Nations:** 39 (+146 custom tribe names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 8
