The **Doomsday Clock** is a battle-royale style game modifier added in [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0). It is part of the public modifier rotation, so it can turn up in public games, and it can be enabled in private lobbies at four speeds: **Slow, Normal, Fast and Very Fast**.

## How it works {#How_it_works}

The clock is shown as a slider in the upper-right corner of the screen. As the match goes on, the share of the map a side must hold rises in waves; a 10-minute grace period holds the clock off at the start of the game, and the squeeze ramps up later on to break late-game stalemates. Any side that falls below the bar is _doomed_ and gets the **Doomsday debuff**:

1. A 30-second warning ("Danger, decay in X s") with a blinking skull.
2. [Troops](/Troops) slowly drain, down to a floor of **5% of maximum** rather than to zero, so a brief dip below the bar is recoverable — the drain stops the moment the side climbs back above it.
3. [Warships](/Warship) bleed too, gently at first and then sharply, until they effectively become one-shot kills.
4. If the side stays doomed, its **territory decays** into radiated wilderness from the edges in and its structures detonate, until it is fully eliminated roughly two and a half minutes after the skull first appeared.

## Details {#Details}

- Doomed warships stay out on patrol instead of idling at their [ports](/Port).
- In team games, teams are judged against the same bar as solo sides.
- As of [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0), the clock is deactivated once the game has a winner.
- The Doomsday Clock is separate from **Overtime**, the anti-stalemate rule in public [FFA](/Free_for_All) where the win threshold _drops_ over time.
