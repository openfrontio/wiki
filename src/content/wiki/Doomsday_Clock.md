The **Doomsday Clock** is a battle-royale style zone game modifier added in [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0). It is part of the public modifier rotation, so it can turn up in public games.

## How it works {#How_it_works}

A shrinking doom zone closes in over the course of a match, and the sides judged to be _doomed_ steadily lose [troops](/Troops) — and their [warships](/Warship). A 10-minute grace period holds the clock off at the start of the game, and a wave-based squeeze ramps up later on to break late-game stalemates.

## Details {#Details}

- The troop drain is floored at 5% of a side's maximum rather than wiping it straight to zero, so being doomed cripples a side without instantly deleting it.
- [Warships](/Warship) decay alongside troops for doomed sides, at a gentler-but-steeper rate of attrition.
- Doomed warships stay out on patrol instead of idling at their [ports](/Port).
- In team games, teams are judged against the same bar as solo sides.
