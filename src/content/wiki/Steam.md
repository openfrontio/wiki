**OpenFront on Steam** is the desktop client of [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io), launched on **September 17th 2026** with [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0). It is available from the [OpenFront Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3560670/OpenFront/).

OpenFront is, and will always remain, free to play in the browser, with full cross-platform multiplayer and server parity. The Steam client is the same game with a set of things a browser tab can't do, and buying it directly funds the open-source game's development.

## Steam perks {#Steam_perks}

- **Permanent verified name.**
- **Permanent custom lobby creation** permissions (boosting and queueing a lobby still cost the usual amount).
- **Instant Trusted account status**: Steam accounts skip the trust ladder, so you can join trusted-only lobbies straight away and play games that anonymous griefers can't drop into.
- **One month of subscription included** (a $9.99 value). If you already have an active web subscription, the Steam month is stacked on top of your balance.
- **Exclusive cosmetic packs**, including a new exclusive skin every month. Since [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) a shared singleplayer save also opens from its link or code on Steam, and a subscription in its grace period shows as active in your account.
- **Exclusive Discord rank**, linked with `/link-steam` (see [Community Roles](/Community_Roles)).
- **Offline and local play**, and maps and assets pre-cached on disk so games start without waiting on a download.
- **Steam achievements, trading cards and profile banners.**
- **A full sound and music rework**, with a looping menu theme, a looping gameplay track and new commissioned sound effects.
- **A +50% XP boost** while your Steam Plus subscription is active, from [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) (see [Levels and XP](/Levels_and_XP)).
- Ad-free.

## Linking your account {#Linking_your_account}

Linking doesn't create a second [account](/Accounts): your stats, cosmetics and active subscription carry over from your existing OpenFront account to Steam, or the other way round if you start on Steam and later play in the browser.

- You can **sign in with Steam on the web**, or link Steam from the website.
- Linking requires a real account, not just a session.
- Discord and Google login on the desktop build are routed through the browser link flow.
- Steam's locale is used as the default game [language](/Languages).
- Since [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0), subscribing while you still have free Steam days adds those days to the end of your subscription.

## Desktop features {#Desktop_features}

- Steam rich presence and invite handling: an **Invite Friends** button in the desktop lobby (the web lobby host has one too), and a mid-match Steam invite navigates you straight to the game.
- The Steam shell version is shown beside the game version, with the full version string in the footer.
- An **Exit game** control in the nav.
- Every join path (including the Detailed View lobby browser) is gated while a Steam update is pending.

## Roadmap {#Roadmap}
