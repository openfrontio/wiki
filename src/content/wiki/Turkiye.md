**Türkiye** is a country map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0), credited in the release notes to **halil1054** and **RickD004** — the whole of Türkiye with real elevation, its major rivers and lakes, and the Bosphorus and Dardanelles open to ships, so the map spans both Europe and Asia.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Turkiye_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="288" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="2352" data-file-height="1060"><figcaption>The Türkiye map.</figcaption></figure>

The Anatolian plateau is high and mountainous, so inland expansion is slow, while the Aegean, Mediterranean and Black Sea coasts are faster going and give plenty of room for [ports](/Port). The two straits let [warships](/Warship) and [trade ships](/Trade_Ship) pass between the Black Sea and the Aegean.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 30 named [nations](/Nations) based on Turkish provinces, including Istanbul, Edirne, Çanakkale, Bursa, Balikesir, Izmir, Mugla, Denizli, Afyonkarahisar, Eskisehir, Van, Kars and Hakkari.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Countries, Europe, Asia
- **Nations:** 30
- **Public rotation frequency:** not set in the map data
