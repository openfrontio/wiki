Update 21.0, previously named Update 0.21.0, was released on the 6th of April 2025, added various new features, most notably the Team mode, Multilingual support and multiple new maps.

## New Features {#New_Features}

### Team Mode {#Team_Mode}

Red vs. Blue team, a team wins if they occupy more than 80% of the terrain. There is also a gray bot team. Members in the same [clan](/Clan) (with [CLAN] in front of their name) will always be placed in the same team.

### Gameplay {#Gameplay}

- Players can now donate [gold](/Gold).
- A game option to disable [nukes](/Atom_Bomb) has been added.
- [Missile silos](/Missile_Silo) now have a cooldown.

### New Maps {#New_Maps}

- [Between Two Seas](/Between_Two_Seas)
- [Japan and Neighbors](/East_Asia)
- [Known World](/Known_World)
- [Australia](/Australia)
- [Iceland](/Iceland)

### Revised Maps {#Revised_Maps}

- [North America](/North_America)
  - Slightly smaller size
  - Improved river and lake systems
  - Mississippi now connects to northern lakes with 3+ exits to the sea
- [Africa](/Africa)
  - Slightly smaller size
  - Improved river and lake systems
  - Great Rift Valley lake system now linked with Congo basin and rivers to the south
  - Main river network has 3 exits to the sea
- [Europe](/Europe)
  - Rivers and lakes implemented across the continent
  - The entire North African coast is now included (full Mediterranean)
  - Iceland has been removed
  - Additional nation bots
  - Improved coastline details
- [MENA](/MENA)
  - Nile, Tigris and Euphrates rivers implemented
  - Improved coastline details
  - The map bounds have been extended further west and restricted slightly in the north
- [South America](/South_America)
  - Amazon river basin implemented, connected to the Orinoco basin with 2 exits to the sea
  - Rio de la Plata basin implemented
  - Added coastline details
  - Added the map to the rotation

### Flags {#Flags}

- 43 new flags
- Improvements to 9 existing flags

### Combat & Military {#Combat_&_Military}

- Nukes now properly reduce attacking [troops](/Troops) and [transport ships](/Transport_Ship), consistent with their effect on other population. (#350)
- [SAM launchers](/SAM_Launcher) now avoid targeting the same nuke twice, improving their defensive capability. (#270)
- The [warship](/Warship) shell lifetime has been increased to 2 seconds after the death of the warship that shot the shell, preventing unfair advantages when a ship backs away while fighting. (#354)

### Trade & Economy {#Trade_&_Economy}

- Trading with allied [ports](/Port) and nearby ports is now prioritized:
  - 2x more likely to trade with ports belonging to an ally OR those close by.
  - 3x more likely to trade with ports that are both allied AND close by. (#335)
- Trade embargoes are now automatically created when the player is attacked by other players, adding strategic consequences to aggression. (#353)

### Performance Improvements {#Performance_Improvements}

- Fixed a bug causing nations to spawn too frequently.
- Various other performance enhancements.

### UI {#UI}

- Added the unit count to the [construction menu](/Buildings).
- The emoji table has been updated.

### Tools {#Tools}

A (external) map GUI editer has been created.

## Bug Fixes {#Bug_Fixes}

- Aspect ratio issues with US state flags have been fixed.
- Fixed an issue that could prevent lobbies from starting when the max player count has been exceeded.
