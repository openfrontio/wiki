**Paris** is a European city map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) — the city limits of Paris, with the Seine, the canals and the main boulevards drawn as waterways, and the city's quartiers as nations. Credited in the release notes to **Théophile** and **RickD004**.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Paris_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="353" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="2436" data-file-height="1344"><figcaption>The Paris map.</figcaption></figure>

Because the boulevards are water, the map plays like a dense grid of islands: [ports](/Port) and [warships](/Warship) matter even though the whole map is one city, and the Seine and the two canals are the main shipping lanes.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 60 named [nations](/Nations) based on the quartiers of Paris, including Amérique, Archives, Arsenal, Arts-et-Métiers, Auteuil, Batignolles, Bel-Air, Belleville, Bercy, Chaillot and Île de la Cité. A further 50 custom tribe names are used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Europe, New
- **Nations:** 60 (+50 custom tribe names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** not set in the map data
