**Brain Rot** is an arcade map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0), credited in the release notes to **PatrickPlaysBadly**, **VariableVince** and **RickD004** — a map shaped like a brain, with nations named after its regions and functions. It is a permanent addition, and was boosted in public rotation for Halloween. [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) gave it new, calmer decoration art now that Halloween is over.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 43 named [nations](/Nations), including Frontal Lobe, Occipital Lobe, Cerebellum, Brain Stem, Short-term Memory and Creativity.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Arcade, New
- **Nations:** 43
- **Public rotation frequency:** 50
