[Map](/Maps) of South America, Central America, Florida, and the Caribbean.

Mostly flat and mountainous, with some hills mostly in Brazil.

Decent amount of water and has the Panama canal, in addition to rivers, making the [map](/Maps) good for [trade ships](/Trade_Ship).
