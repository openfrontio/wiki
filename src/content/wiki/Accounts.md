An **account** lets you play OpenFront under a persistent identity. [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0) added account-level usernames, a verified badge and Login with Google; [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0) made all profiles public, added Sign in with [Steam](/Steam), trusted-only lobbies, player reporting, Support a Creator and self-service account deletion.

## Usernames and verified badge {#Usernames_and_verified_badge}

- You can claim an account-level username in the form `base.suffix` (subscribers can claim a bare name with no suffix). If a bare name is unavailable, the game explains the suffixed name you get instead.
- A verified badge is shown at the top-right corner of your name on player-facing lists. Since Update 34.0 the verified name is on by default for eligible subscribers, and you are warned when a reserved name is about to run out.
- A verified-name toggle lets you choose to play under your account name.
- Click a player name to copy it; a full profile URL can be pasted anywhere a player ID is expected.

## Sign-in options {#Sign-in_options}

- **Google** — sign in with a Google account and link it to an existing account; once linked, the account shows "Linked to Google".
- **Discord** — Discord login is also available.
- **Steam** — as of Update 34.0 you can sign in with Steam on the web, or link Steam from the website. Linking requires a real account (not just a session) and does not create a second account: stats, cosmetics and any active subscription carry over. See [Steam](/Steam).

## Profiles and stats {#Profiles_and_stats}

- Every profile is **public** as of Update 34.0, so anyone can review a player's games — the same approach Lichess and Chess.com use to prevent cheating and teaming.
- A shareable player profile shows your game history and includes a [Clans](/Clan) tab, and can be linked to directly. History cards have a game-link button and copyable game IDs.
- The account modal has a Games tab, and stats live in a dedicated stats modal (previously called "ranking").
- The nav has a profile dropdown menu, a bell button for news, and a mobile account icon.

## Trust tiers, trusted lobbies and reporting {#Trust_tiers_trusted_lobbies_and_reporting}

Accounts have a **trust tier** that grows over time. Since Update 34.0:

- Some scheduled public games are **trusted-only**, gated on account trust tier, with lock indicators on their lobby cards; the listing names the tier required. Open lobbies are always available alongside them, and v0.34.1 reduced how many lobbies are trusted-only. Signed-out players are told to sign in when blocked from a trusted lobby.
- [Steam](/Steam) accounts get instant trusted status and skip the trust ladder.
- A **Report Player** button in the player panel lets you report botting, teaming, bad names or griefing straight into the moderation tools. Banned players are shown the reason for their ban.

## Support a Creator {#Support_a_Creator}

Add your favourite [content creator's](/Content_creators) code to your profile and they receive a share of every purchase you make. Creators get their own profile panel and `openfront.io/c/CODE` share links, and the code survives every sign-in flow.

## Deleting your account {#Deleting_your_account}

Accounts can be deleted from account settings. Deletion is queued for 24 hours so you can change your mind; once it goes through, all of your games are hidden.
