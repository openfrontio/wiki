An **account** lets you play OpenFront under a persistent identity. [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0) added account-level usernames, a verified badge and Login with Google.

## Usernames and verified badge {#Usernames_and_verified_badge}

- You can claim an account-level username in the form `base.suffix` (subscribers can claim a bare name with no suffix).
- A verified badge is shown next to your account name on player-facing lists.
- A verified-name toggle lets you choose to play under your account name.

## Login with Google {#Login_with_Google}

You can sign in with a Google account and link it to an existing account; once linked, the account shows "Linked to Google".

## Profiles and stats {#Profiles_and_stats}

- A shareable player profile shows your game history and includes a [Clans](/Clan) tab, and can be linked to directly.
- The account modal has a Games tab, and stats now live in a dedicated stats modal (previously called "ranking").
