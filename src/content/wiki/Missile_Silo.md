The **Missile Silo** is a weaponry [building](/Buildings) costing 1 million [gold](/Gold). It is used to launch [nukes](/Nuke).

# Usage {#Usage}

The only purpose of the **Missile Silo** is to launch missiles. To launch a missile, first right click on the targeted and select the building menu. Then chose between the three types of missile, [Atom Bomb](/Atom_Bomb), [Hydrogen Bomb](/Hydrogen_Bomb), or [MIRV](/MIRV). The missile will be launched by the nearest **Missile Silo**.

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/198px-Rl_Silo_from_upclose.png" decoding="async" width="198" height="182" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="473" data-file-height="435"><figcaption>A reloading <b>Missile Silo.</b></figcaption></figure>

When a **Missile Silo** launches a missile it becomes red for 8 seconds, indicating reloading. While reloading, it cannot be used to launch any nukes. As of [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0), launching a [MIRV](/MIRV) also puts the silo on cooldown (previously MIRVs were exempt).
