Update 19.0, previously named update 0.19.0, deployed on the 16th of March 2025, added various new features, including the [SAM](/SAM_Launcher) and the replay system.

## New Features {#New_Features}

- [SAM](/SAM_Launcher) (Surface-to-Air Missile System). This new [building](/Buildings) has the ability to shoot down nearby missiles.
- New Map: [Britannia](/Britannia)
- Games can now be replayed after they've ended, simply by following the openfront.io/join/id link.
- The game will now alert the player if they are desynced.
- There is now a maximum player count per map:
  - Small [Maps](/Maps) (e.g. Europe): 60 players
  - Medium [Maps](/Maps): 70 players
  - Large [Maps](/Maps) (e.g. World): 150 players

## Balancing Changes {#Balancing_Changes}

- Increased trade opportunities when global [port](/Port) count is low.
- It is now easier to attack [irradiated](/Fallout) land (nuclear wasteland) when 70% of the [map](/Maps) is nuked.
- Reduced bonus advantages for larger nations.

## AI Improvements {#AI_Improvements}

- Improved embargo handling by NPC [nations](/Nations).
- NPCs will now embargo enemy nations.
- NPC relations will deteriorate if the player places them under embargo.
