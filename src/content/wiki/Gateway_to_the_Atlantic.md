_Not to be confused with [Strait of Gibraltar](/Strait_of_Gibraltar)_

Gateway to the Antlantic is a [map](/Maps) with a canal in the middle (naturally-made). There are two lands, one in the north and one in the south. Europe is on the north side, and Africa on the south side.

## Nations {#Map_nations}

The map features 27 named [nations](/Nations), including Swiss Confederation, Duchy of Burgundy, Duchy of Aquitaine, County of Provence, Kingdom of Navarre and Kingdom of Valencia.
