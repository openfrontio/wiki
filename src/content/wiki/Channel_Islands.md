**Channel Islands** is a North American map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0) by **crunchybbb** — the Southern California coast from Los Angeles down to San Diego, with the Channel Islands scattered offshore.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 44 named [nations](/Nations), including Huntington Beach, Newport Beach, Laguna Beach, San Clemente, San Juan Capistrano and Santa Catalina.
