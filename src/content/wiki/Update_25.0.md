[OpenFront v.25.0](/OpenFront.io)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> was released August 20, 2025.

# 📦 [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) v25.0 Changelog {#📦_OpenFront_v25.0_Changelog}

## ⚙️ Major Changes {#⚙️_Major_Changes}

- Removed workers & troop ratio bar
- Players earn 1k [gold](/Gold) per second, bots earn 500 [gold](/Gold) per second.
- Added [Factories](/Factory) & [Trains](/Train) to public matches
- [Trade ship](/Trade_Ship) spawn rate is determined by the number of [ports](/Port) players have and total number of [trade ships](/Trade_Ship) on the [map](/Maps).
- [Traitor](/Traitor) debuff does not apply when [betraying](/Traitor) an inactive player
- Added [SAM](/SAM_Launcher) smart targeting - predicts incoming [nuke](/Nuke)'s trajectory, allowing early missile launch before targets is in range
- Fixed hanging [alliance](/Ally) request bug
- Fixed the disconnected (Zzz) icon from persisting when player reconnects
- [Ports](/Port) and [Factories](/Factory) share same price scaling
- Large players get a defense debuff

## 🗺️ Maps {#🗺️_Maps}

- Added [Pluto](/Pluto) map to public and singleplayer matches
- Made [Baikal](/Baikal_(Nuke_Wars)) more frequent in public matches rotation

## 🧪 UI & Quality of Life {#🧪_UI_&_Quality_of_Life}

- Added FPS monitor overview
- Players on the same [team](/Teams) get a distinct color based on team color
- Added kick player functionality in private games

# Minor Releases {#Minor_Releases}

### [v25.1](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/releases/tag/v0.25.1) {#v25.1}

_August 21, 2025._

#### **[Factories](/Factory) & [Rail Networks](/Railroad)** {#Factories_&_Rail_Networks}

- [Factories](/Factory) now automatically build [train tracks](/Railroad) connecting to:
  - Other [factories](/Factory)
  - [Cities](/City)
  - [Ports](/Port)
- [Trains](/Train) spawn at [factories](/Factory) and deliver goods to [cities](/City) and [ports](/Port)
- [Trade](/Trade) Revenue (per [train](/Train)):
  - [Allied](/Ally) territories: 50,000 [gold](/Gold)
  - Neighboring territories: 25,000 [gold](/Gold)
  - Your own territory: 10,000 [gold](/Gold)

Connect your [rail](/Railroad) network with [allies](/Ally) and neighbors for maximum profit!

#### **Major Changes** {#Major_Changes}

- Removed workers & troop ratio bar
- Players earn 1k [gold](/Gold) per second, [bots](/Bots) earn 500 [gold](/Gold) per second.
- [Trade ship](/Trade_Ship) spawn rate is determined by the number of [ports](/Port) players have and total number of [trade ships](/Trade_Ship) on the map.
- [Traitor](/Traitor) debuff does not apply when [betraying](/Traitor) an inactive player
- Added [SAM](/SAM_Launcher) smart targeting - predicts incoming [nuke](/Nuke)'s trajectory, allowing early missile launch before targets is in range
- Fixed hanging [alliance](/Ally) request bug
- Fixed the disconnected (Zzz) icon from persisting when player reconnects
- [Ports](/Port) and [Factories](/Factory) share same price scaling
- Large players get a defense debuff

#### **Maps** {#Maps}

- Added [Pluto](/Pluto) map to public and singleplayer matches
- Made [Baikal](/Baikal_(Nuke_Wars)) more frequent in public matches rotation
- Added Yenisei

#### **UI & Quality of Life** {#UI_&_Quality_of_Life}

- Added FPS monitor overview
- Players on the same [team](/Teams) get a distinct color based on [team](/Teams) color
- Added kick player functionality in private games

### [**v25.4**](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/releases/tag/v0.25.4) {#v25.4}

_August 25, 2025._

- Halved trade ship spawn rate but doubled the gold per trade ship to reduce CPU usage and lag.
- Team games only: Reduced gold generated by trains from 50k to 25k for teammates.
- Created stations regardless of factory ownership.
- Fixed bug where SAMs didn't intercept nukes fired from long distances.
