To ally a person in the mode [Free for All](/Free_for_All) you right click their territory, and select the handshake option.

If the player accepts, you become allies for 5 minutes.

# Details {#Details}

In [Free for All](/Free_for_All) you can [betray](/Traitor) and ally freely. Other players can not see who you are allied with in this mode.

It works differently in the [Teams](/Teams) mode. In this mode you are always allied with your selected color and you are unable to [betray](/Traitor) your allies. The game tries to put your [clan](/Clan) members in your [team](/Teams).

Being [allies](/Ally) has multiple effects:

- [Trade](/Trade) is prioritized between allies.
- You do not accidentally attack an ally with ground units or [transport ships](/Transport_Ship).
- Your [warships](/Warship) no longer capture their [trade ships](/Trade_Ship).
- You can send troops or [gold](/Gold) to reinforce your ally.

Breaking an [alliance](/Ally) causes the status [Traitor](/Traitor):

- Global 50% defense and 20% attack speed reduction for 30 seconds.
- Allies can break their alliance with the [traitor](/Traitor) without triggering the [traitor](/Traitor) status.
- An embargo is made, blocking [trades](/Trade) from being done until you either become allies again, or right click the player and select to allow [trade](/Trade).
- The number of [betrayals](/Traitor) on your information panel will increase by one.
