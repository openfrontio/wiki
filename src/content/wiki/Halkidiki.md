A [map](/Maps) based on where Greece meets the Aegan Sea in the north.

It features three fingers of land, with plenty of water around it.

## Nations {#Map_nations}

The map features 8 named [nations](/Nations), including Mount Athos, Kassandra, Sithonia, Thessaloniki, Polygyros and Kilkis.
