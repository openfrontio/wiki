This [map](/Maps) consists of Antarctica without ice.

This leads to the landmass becoming a huge archipelago as the ice no longer connects the landmasses together, making it the map with the most islands in the entire game.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Deglaciated_Antarctica_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="512" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="2300" data-file-height="1840"><figcaption>The Deglaciated Antarctica map, from the <a href="https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO/tree/main/map-generator/assets/maps/deglaciatedantarctica" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">OpenFront game files</a>.</figcaption></figure>

## Nations {#Map_nations}

The map features 9 named [nations](/Nations), including Penguin Empire, Norwegian Claim, Upper Australian Claim, Lower Australian Claim, Argentinian Claim and United Kingdom Claim.
