[Map](/Maps) of Oceania and part of South East Asia. This [map](/Maps) is mostly water. Mostly plains and hills with some mountains mainly in the northwest.

The east of the [map](/Maps) is mostly ocean but there are some small islands which makes it good for [trade](/Trade) via [ports](/Port).
