[OpenFront.io](/OpenFront.io) contains many interesting and diverse maps which creates a different playing style for each individual map. Some maps are more built for slow expansion, some for quick expansion, some for trade and some for quicker or slower games. A game of [Openfront.io](/OpenFront.io) is complete when 80% or more of the map has been conquered by one player.

<div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">See also the <a href="https://www.openfront-atlas.xyz/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">OpenFront Atlas</a>, a community interactive browser of every OpenFront map.</div>

## Continental Maps {#Continental_Maps}

### [World](/World) {#World}

Map of the World (sometimes referred to as the globe or Earth map) is a map that shows all of the 7 major continents of the world as well as all 5 major oceans of the world. This also included islands such as the Caribbean islands, the British isles, Iceland, New Zealand, Madagascar, Japan and others. The map's [terrain](/Terrain) is comprised of an even amount of flat, hilly and mountainous terrain that is quite evenly spread through the map. However, Asia tends to be more mountainous, Africa tends to be more hilly and South America tends to be more flat. However, it should be noted that most of the map of water and with the Panama and Suez canals being a thing, this results in this being one of the best maps for trade and [warships](/Warship).

### [Giant World Map](/Giant_World_Map) {#Giant_World_Map}

A giant map of the World. The map's [terrain](/Terrain) is comprised of an even amount of flat, hilly and mountainous terrain that is quite evenly spread through the map. However, Asia tends to be more mountainous, Africa tends to be more hilly and South America tends to be more flat. It should be noted that most of the map is made of water and with the Panama Canal being a thing (the Suez Canal does not exist on the Giant World Map), this results in this being one of the best maps for trade and [warships](/Warship).

### [North America](/North_America) {#North_America}

Map of Northern and Central America and Greenland. Mostly hilly and mountainous [terrain](/Terrain) with some plains mostly near oceans and in the northern and eastern parts of the map. There are a lot of water and rivers.

### [South America](/South_America) {#South_America}

Map of South America, Central America, Florida, and the Caribbean. Mostly flat and mountainous, with some hills mostly in Brazil. Decent amount of water and has the Panama canal, in addition to rivers, making the map good for trade.

### [Europe](/Europe) {#Europe}

Map consisting of all continental Europe, as well as nearby islands such as: Great Britain, Ireland, Iceland, Corsica, Sardinia and more. It also shows parts of north Africa and a small part of west Asia. The map's [Terrain](/Terrain) is mostly flat in the eastern and central regions of the map. However, Scandinavia and the Alps are quite mountainous regions, with areas such as Türkiye and Spain being quite hilly and mountainous. It doesn't include Iceland because the map is a bit stretched compared to the old one. However, it should be noted that while nearly all of the map (except the western side) is land, but due to the Mediterranean Sea and Baltic Sea, and rivers, it is quite a great map for [ports](/Port).

### [Europe Classic](/Europe_Classic) {#Europe_Classic}

This map is a duplicate of the older [Europe](/Europe) map without rivers. It is not stretched out like the other maps in the game as well. This map has been added because some players’ devices don’t have enough resources to play the new map, and also because the new map excludes Iceland.

### [Asia](/Asia) {#Asia}

Map of all of continental Asia, as well as it's nearby islands, such as Japan, Indonesia, the Philippines and Sri Lanka. It also shows parts of east Africa as well as eastern Europe. The map's [terrain](/Terrain) is quite flat in the north, but in the central regions of the map (such as Tibet/west China and Kazakhstan) there are mountains that are difficult to invade through. However, most of the western side of the map is hilly with a few sparse mountains. Areas other than the north that are also flat are: Indonesia, India, east China and south east Asia. However, it should be noted that most of the map is land, but due to long coastlines and islands, building [ports](/Port) is a great strategy.

### [Africa](/Africa) {#Africa}

Map of the African continent with parts of the Italian peninsula, Middle East, and Mediterranean. [Terrain](/Terrain) is very hilly and mountainous, making spread slow. Africa also has long rivers allowing for trade farther inland via a [port](/Port).

### [Oceania](/Oceania) {#Oceania}

Map of Oceania and part of South East Asia. This map is mostly water. Mostly plains and hills with some mountains mainly in the northwest. The east of the map is mostly ocean but there are some small islands which makes it good for trade via [ports](/Port).

## Regional Maps {#Regional_Maps}

### [Black Sea](/Black_Sea) {#Black_Sea}

Map of areas surrounding the Black Sea. Mountains everywhere with most of the plains in the northwest and southeast. Center of the map is dominated by water and there are several rivers going across the map. It has the island of Crimea, but most of the map is land. So, it is somewhat harder to attack in the map outside of Crimea. Most of the map is mountain: the flattest part is in the Southern District area of Russia.

### [Britannia](/Britannia) {#Britannia}

Map of the British Isles (Great Britain and Ireland) with a segment of northern France (the Pas-de-Calais).

This map also features historic British kingdoms and lordships (making it the one with the coolest flags!).

### [Gateway to the Atlantic](/Gateway_to_the_Atlantic) {#Gateway_to_the_Atlantic}

Map of Spain, France, Gibraltar, and Northwest Africa. It has lots of rivers. The nations there include French and Spanish provinces, as well as ones with names such as "Baguettes",  "An anti pope", “Wine”, and “Sardines,” referencing French cuisine and history.

### [Between Two Seas](/Between_Two_Seas) {#Between_Two_Seas}

Map of the mountainous region between and around the Caspian sea and the Black Sea.

### [Iceland](/Iceland) {#Iceland}

Map of the country Iceland. It's  mountainous and it has a coastline riddled with fjords and inlets. That makes it not only suitable for land attacks but also for trade.

### [East Asia](/East_Asia) {#East_Asia}

This is a map with a large amounts of water and two large landmasses: Kyushu and the Korean Peninsula. Previously named [Japan and Neighbors](/East_Asia).

### [MENA](/MENA) {#MENA}

Map of Middle East and North Africa, with some parts of Southern Europe visible. Mostly hilly [terrain](/Terrain) with most of the mountains present in the northern part of the map. There are some water and rivers.

### [Australia](/Australia) {#Australia}

Map of the country Australia. It's especially suitable for land attacks, as the map is one big island and one smaller island named Tasmania.

### [Faroe Islands](/Faroe_Islands) {#Faroe_Islands}

Map of the [Faroe Islands](/Faroe_Islands), a real life island cluster located in the north Atlantic sea, between Iceland and Great Britain. It is currently under the Kingdom of Denmark. The map itself presents a lot of waterways and it is a good map for naval invasions and trading via [ports](/Port).

### [Falkland Islands](/Falkland_Islands) {#Falkland_Islands}

Map of the [Falkland Islands](/Falkland_Islands) located outside of Argentina.

It consists of two main islands, surrounded by water on all sides. There are plenty of tiny islands scattered around the main islands.

### [Baikal](/Baikal_(Nuke_Wars)) {#Baikal}

Map of Lake [Baikal](/Baikal_(Nuke_Wars)) in Siberia, Russia.

It features two sides of the lake, with a large body of water between. In the [teams](/Teams) mode this map can tend to become a slow fight fought mostly with [nukes](/Nuke) as the shore can be well protected with [warships](/Warship).

### [Halkidiki](/Halkidiki) {#Halkidiki}

A map based on where Greece meets the Aegean Sea in the north.

It features three fingers of land, with plenty of water around it.

### [Italia](/Italia) {#Italia}

Map of Italy, Sicily, Sardinia, Parts of Balkans, and Parts of the Iberian peninsula. This is great for land attacks, since mostly is just on one land. But when on islands, [warships](/Warship) are great for attacking.

### Yenisei {#Yenisei}

A mess of rivers, land, and islands.

### [Strait of Gibraltar](/Strait_of_Gibraltar) {#Strait_of_Gibraltar}

<div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">Not to be confused with <a href="/Gateway_to_the_Atlantic" title="Gateway to the Atlantic">Gateway to the Atlantic</a>.</div>

Map of the Strait of Gibraltar.

Two pieces of land separated by the naturally occurring [Strait of Gibraltar](/Strait_of_Gibraltar).

### Montreal {#Montreal}

A map of a city in Canada home to 1.7 million people. It has some Islands and water.

### [New York City](/New_York_City) {#New_York_City}

This is a map of New York City, home to 8 million people, has 5 peices of land (boroughs) Manhattan, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island, and Brooklyn. Bronx is cut off in this map, but it shows some other parts of Long Island and New Jersey. The most islandous are south of the Long Island. Governors Island is also shown in the middle.

## Arcade Maps {#Arcade_Maps}

As of [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0), arcade maps include **More than Luck**, **Branching Paths** and **Las Vegas Strip** (see [Added in Update 33.0](#New_maps_in_v33)), and arcade maps now appear in all playlists. Since [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0), [Labyrinth](/Labyrinth) turns up less often in public rotation, and [Chopping Block](/Chopping_Block) and the cosmic map [Luna](/Luna) were removed from public rotation entirely.

## Other Maps {#Other_Maps}

### [Pangaea](/Pangaea) {#Pangaea}

Map of the old continent Pangaea, which already broke apart in real life. It has a few rivers here and there. This map is optimal for land attacks, as there is only one continent, and it is easy to attack other parts of it.

### [Mars](/Mars) {#Mars}

A map of what Mars might've looked like if it had water on its surface.

### [Deglaciated Antarctica](/Deglaciated_Antarctica) {#Deglaciated_Antarctica}

This map consists of Antarctica without ice. This leads to the landmass becoming a huge archipelago as the ice no longer connects the landmasses together, making it the map with the most island in the entire game.

### [Pluto](/Pluto) {#Pluto}

A map of the planet Pluto if it was converted into water and land. There is a bunch of islands at the north, so it will be great for boat shipping thing whatever.

## Unused Maps {#Unused_Maps}

This section is dedicated for maps that have previously been available, but no longer are.

It could be related to tournaments, events, or maps that are retired.

### [Known World](/Known_World) {#Known_World}

This is a map that has been taken out from the available list of maps to play on. It was previously listed under "Other Maps".

It contained two bodies of land both with surrounding water, based on the book series A Song of Ice and Fire by George R.R. Martin that is also the inspiration for _Game of Thrones_.

# Maps and Images {#Maps_and_Images}

The [height maps](/Height_Map) are [terrain](/Terrain) maps. These maps show water as grey-white. The colors are different depending on the [terrain](/Terrain), with green for plains, Khaki or Tan for highlands and desert, and white for snow, ice, and mountains. The images can update to remove smaller blue things and add green, white, or Khaki or tan things that got erased by the white.
