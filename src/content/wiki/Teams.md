Teams is a game mode putting players into different teams, up to 7. The more players/[bots](/Bots), the more countries per one team, but the game still limits the number of [bots](/Bots) in the world to 500 or so.

You get assigned a color, and play together with your color to beat the other teams.

Unlike [Free for All](/Free_for_All) you do not get to break your [alliance](/Ally) or [betray](/Traitor) them. However, it is still possible to do team damage using missiles.

In the team game mode players often decide to pool resources, like sending troops to their [allies](/Ally) on the front line or send [gold](/Gold) to be able to afford [defense posts](/Defense_Post) and later on in the game [MIRVs](/MIRV).

## Winning {#Winning}

As of [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0), team games use the same **80%** land win threshold as [Free for All](/Free_for_All) (previously a team needed 95% of the map).

## Team names {#Team_names}

Since [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0), [clan](/Clan) tags can be used as team names: a team made up mostly of one clan takes that clan's tag, a team split between two clans gets a hybrid two-clan name, and other teams keep their normal names.

Update 34.0 also fixed duo/trio/quad team forming so that human players are placed together instead of being scattered across bot teams, and fixed one-player teams on small public maps.
