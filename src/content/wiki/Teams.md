Teams is a game mode putting players into different teams, up to 7. The more players/[bots](/Bots), the more countries per one team, but the game still limits the number of [bots](/Bots) in the world to 500 or so.Template:Fix/category

You get assigned a color, and play together with your color to beat the other teams.

Unlike [Free for All](/Free_for_All) you do not get to break your [alliance](/Ally) or [betray](/Traitor) them. However, it is still possible to do team damage using missiles.

In the team game mode players often decide to pool resources, like sending troops to their [allies](/Ally) on the front line or send [gold](/Gold) to be able to afford [defense posts](/Defense_Post) and later on in the game [MIRVs](/MIRV).
