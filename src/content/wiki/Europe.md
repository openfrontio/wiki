Europe is a [map](/Maps) consisting of all continental Europe, as well as nearby islands such as: Great Britain, Ireland, Iceland, Corsica, Sardinia and more.

It also shows parts of north Africa and a small part of west Asia.

The [map](/Maps)'s [Terrain](/Terrain) is mostly flat in the eastern and central regions of the [map](/Maps). However, Scandinavia and the Alps are quite mountainous regions, with areas such as Türkiye and Spain being quite hilly and mountainous.

Due to the Mediterranean Sea and Baltic Sea, and rivers, it is a great map for [ports](/Port).

## Nations {#Map_nations}

The map features 52 named [nations](/Nations), including Ireland, England, Spain, France, Italy and Switzerland.

## Technical {#Technical}

The size of the [map](/Maps) is 2300 x 1647.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>
