Besides in-game [gold](/Gold), which only exists inside a match, OpenFront has two account-level **currencies**: **Plutonium**, the hard currency bought with real money, and **Caps**, the soft currency earned by playing.

## Plutonium {#Plutonium}

Plutonium is bought in the store (in packs or as a custom amount) and spent on cosmetics, bundles, [tribe](/Tribes) names and boosts. You can also earn it:

- **Level milestones** — the [progression](/Levels_and_XP) reward track pays Plutonium every 25 levels. Level Plutonium is held until your [account](/Accounts) reaches Trusted status.
- **Subscriptions** — [Steam Plus](/Steam) pays 250 Plutonium on each monthly renewal plus 10 Plutonium a day; the web subscription tiers include a monthly bonus and a daily reward that scale with tier.
- **Clan stores** — a cosmetic added to a [clan](/Clan) store costs its members 100 Plutonium.

## Caps {#Caps}

Caps are awarded at the end of competitive matches, scaled by how much **human opposition** you faced:

<table class="wikitable">
<tr><th>Opposing human teams</th><th>Caps awarded</th></tr>
<tr><td>50 or more</td><td>500</td></tr>
<tr><td>25 or more</td><td>250</td></tr>
<tr><td>10 or more</td><td>100</td></tr>
<tr><td>2 or more</td><td>50</td></tr>
<tr><td>Fewer than 2 (no opposition)</td><td>0</td></tr>
</table>

In [Free for All](/Free_for_All) every other human counts as a team of one. Caps can be donated to your clan's treasury, where leaders and officers spend them on troops for the [clan map](/Clan#Clan_map) (1 cap buys 100 troops).
