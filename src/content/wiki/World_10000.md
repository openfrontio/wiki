**World 10000** is a huge world map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) by **Patrick Plays Badly**, credited in the release notes to **PatrickPlaysBadly**, **VariableVince** and **RickD004**. At 10,000 pixels wide it is by far the largest map in the game, built for custom games and tournaments rather than public rotation, and it is too heavy to run on phones.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/World_10000_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="320" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="10000" data-file-height="5000"><figcaption>The World 10000 map.</figcaption></figure>

The map uses the same rebuilt terrain and waterways as [World 4000](/World_4000) at more than twice the resolution, so every strait, canal and major river is open to [trade ships](/Trade_Ship) and [warships](/Warship), and there is room for several hundred players.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 377 named [nations](/Nations), mostly cities, including Kabul, Algiers, Oran, Annaba, Luanda, Lubango, Huambo, Buenos Aires and Cordoba, down to the Antarctic research stations Neumayer III, Arctowski and Vernadsky. Five extra names honouring the map's makers (Patrick Plays Badly, TriStar, Crunchyb, FloPinguin and Nikola123) are used when a game needs even more.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** World, New
- **Nations:** 377 (+5 reserve names)
- **Size:** 10000 × 5000
- **Public rotation frequency:** not in public rotation (custom games and tournaments)
