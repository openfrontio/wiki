**Canada** is a North American country map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) by **cazlabIO** — Canada from the Great Lakes to Ellesmere Island, with Alaska, Greenland and the United States as impassable terrain.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Canada_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="479" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="3428" data-file-height="2568"><figcaption>The Canada map.</figcaption></figure>

The provinces and territories spawn at their capitals, while the north is shared out between First Nations, Inuit and Métis nations. Hudson Bay, the Arctic archipelago and the Great Lakes give the map a lot of coastline for [ports](/Port) and [warships](/Warship), and the sealed southern border means the only way into the United States is not at all.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 38 named [nations](/Nations): the 13 provinces and territories (British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador, Yukon, Northwest Territories and Nunavut) plus First Nations, Inuit and Métis nations such as Nakoda, Kitikmeot Inuit and Kivalliq Inuit. A further 60 custom tribe names are used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** North America, Countries, New
- **Nations:** 38 (+60 custom tribe names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** not set in the map data
