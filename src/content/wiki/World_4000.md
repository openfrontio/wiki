**World 4000** is a world map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) as the replacement for the retired [Giant World Map](/Giant_World_Map). It is an overhaul of Giant World by **Patrick Plays Badly**, with new terrain, rivers and softer decoration layers, and is credited in the release notes to **PatrickPlaysBadly**, **VariableVince**, **RickD004** and **Josh**.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/World_4000_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="320" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="4120" data-file-height="2060"><figcaption>The World 4000 map.</figcaption></figure>

Giant World started life as a test map and was too laggy for public play when it first shipped, with some major waterways still missing. World 4000 fixes the waterways, so the Panama and Suez canals and the big rivers are all open, which makes it one of the best maps for [trade ships](/Trade_Ship) and [warships](/Warship).

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 111 named [nations](/Nations), including Alaska, Alberta, British Columbia, California, Canada, Greenland, Hawaii, Kansas, Manitoba, Massachusetts, Antarctica and the Western and Eastern Ice Sheets.
