Traitor is a special status, that is obtained by a player, [bot](/Bots) or [nation](/Nations) when they break their [alliance](/Ally), or when they (potentially accidentally) blow up part of an [ally](/Ally)'s territory with a [nuke](/Nuke).

# Details {#Details}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/300px-Traitor_Display.png" decoding="async" width="300" height="229" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="311" data-file-height="237"><figcaption>A player with the <span class="wiki-deadlink">Traitor</span> status active.</figcaption></figure>

The traitor gives a global 50% reduction to your defense when defending against an attack. This means that the attacker loses 50% less troops during an attack. You will also lose 20% attack speed. The traitor debuff lasts for 30 seconds. If you are [allied](/Ally) to a traitor you can break your [alliance](/Ally) with them without becoming a traitor yourself.

This reduction does not only apply to attacks coming from the country you [betrayed](/Ally), but to every other player/[bot](/Bots)/[nation](/Nations) on the map.

See [Combat](/Combat) for more details about the combat formulas for troop loss.
