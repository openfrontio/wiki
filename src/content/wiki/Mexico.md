**Mexico** is a North American country map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0) by **crunchybbb** — the whole country, sandwiched between the Pacific and the [Gulf of Mexico](/Gulf_of_Mexico), with its states as nations.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Mexico_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="461" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="2922" data-file-height="2103"><figcaption>The Mexico map.</figcaption></figure>

Mexico is primarily mountainous, with hot deserts in the north and wet jungles in the south, so expect slow going inland and faster expansion along the coasts.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 32 named [nations](/Nations) — the 31 Mexican states plus Mexico City, each with its own flag — including Baja California, Baja California Sur, Sonora, Chihuahua, Durango, Sinaloa, Coahuila and Tamaulipas.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** North America, Countries, New
- **Nations:** 32
- **Public rotation frequency:** not set in the map data
