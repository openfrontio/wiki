Fallout occurs after any missile lands on ground. It irradiates the ground and leaves fallout.

It is known for its bright green color.

The fallout defense modifier ranges from 2.5× to 5× based on the ratio of fallout tiles on the map. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup>  It can significantly increase army losses.

As the % win condition does not take into account irradiated soil as claimed territory it may trigger a win or loss.
