The official OpenFront [Discord](/Discord) grants a number of **roles** that unlock extra channels and perks. This page lists how to get them.

## Community Artist {#Community_Artist}

React to the role post in **#announcements** to get the Community Artist role. It unlocks the artist lounges, asset feedback and the submission channels for cosmetics. See [Custom Cosmetic Testing](/Custom_Cosmetic_Testing) for how to preview a skin or crown before submitting it.

## Content Creator {#Content_Creator}

You qualify for the [Content Creator](/Content_creators) role with **at least one** of:

- 2,000 views on OpenFront-related videos (views across several videos stack)
- 1,000 or more followers on an active streaming platform
- 20,000 views on YouTube Shorts

Once you are eligible, open a ticket or contact a member of staff to claim the role. Affiliate and Partner tiers sit above it — see [Content creators](/Content_creators).

## Contributor and Lead Contributor {#Contributor_and_Lead_Contributor}

Code contributors to the open-source game on [GitHub](/Github) get roles automatically once their GitHub account is linked to Discord with `/link-github <username>`:

- **10 merged pull requests** → `@Contributor`
- **25 merged pull requests** → `@LeadContributor`

Contributors also qualify for the [OpenFront Dev](https://discord.com/invite/K9zernJB5z) server.

## Image permissions {#Image_permissions}

Posting images needs **Level 3** on the Discord server. Levels come from chatting: about 13–25 messages, counted at most once per minute, gets you there. Content Creators get image permissions immediately.

## Other linked roles {#Other_linked_roles}

- **Steam owners** get an exclusive Discord rank by linking with `/link-steam` — see [Steam](/Steam).
- **Translators** earn a Translator role on the main server by contributing on the [OpenFront Translation](https://discord.com/invite/fDmWy7jrjA) server — see [Languages](/Languages).
