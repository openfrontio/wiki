Discord is the main communication channel chosen by the developers and community.

At the bottom footer on [OpenFront.io](/OpenFront.io) you will find a link to join the official [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) Discord server.

There is a separate Discord for developers. You can find it through the GitHub page for [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io).
