**Ranked** matchmaking pits players against each other in rated games that feed a competitive leaderboard ladder. Ranked **1v1** has been available for some time; [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0) added ranked **2v2** alongside it, with its own ladder.

## Ranked 2v2 {#Ranked_2v2}

- 2v2 has its own leaderboard ladder, shown as a separate leaderboard tab.
- Matchmaking prefers to pair [clanmates](/Clan) as teammates.
- PvP spawn immunity lasts 1 minute.
- Games use compact maps only 50% of the time.
- A game ends early when a full team is dead or disconnected.

## Matchmaking {#Matchmaking}

- The live queue size is shown while you wait, and the client reconnects on silent disconnects.
- Matches that do not fill, or that start short-handed or fail to fully spawn, are cancelled.

## Leaderboards {#Leaderboards}

The 1v1 ranked leaderboard is an individual ladder and no longer shows [clan](/Clan) tags in the rank column; the clan tag is a separate, filterable column.
