This [map](/Maps) is a duplicate of the older [Europe](/Europe) [map](/Maps) without rivers. It is not streched out like the other [maps](/Maps) in the game as well. This [map](/Maps) has been added because some players’ devices don’t have enough resources to play the new [map](/Maps), and also because the new [map](/Maps) excludes Iceland.
