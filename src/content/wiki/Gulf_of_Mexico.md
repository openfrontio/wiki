**Gulf of Mexico** is a North American map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0) by **LounNight** — the whole Gulf basin from Texas and the Mississippi delta round to Florida, Cuba, the Bahamas and the Yucatán.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 37 named [nations](/Nations), including Jamaica, Havana, Santiago de Cuba, Camagüey, The Bahamas and Miami, plus a pool of 50 additional coastal nations (Texas, Louisiana, Florida, Mexican and Cuban cities) that the game can draw on to fill larger lobbies.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** North America, New
- **Nations:** 37 (+50 additional)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 5
