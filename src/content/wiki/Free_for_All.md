Free for All (FFA) is a multiplayer game mode characterized by its competitive structure, in which every player competes individually against all others.

The primary objective is to capture the largest portion of the map. The player who controls the most territory at the end of the match is declared the winner.

A unique feature of this mode is the ability for players to form temporary [alliances](/Ally) with others during gameplay.

However, these [alliances](/Ally) are fragile: attacking an [allied](/Ally) player results in the attacker being marked as a [traitor](/Traitor) for a duration of 30 seconds.

While under this penalty, the [traitor](/Traitor)'s defenses are temporarily weakened, leaving them vulnerable to other players.
