Free for All (FFA) is a multiplayer game mode characterized by its competitive structure, in which every player competes individually against all others.

The primary objective is to capture the largest portion of the map. The player who controls the most territory at the end of the match is declared the winner; a player who holds **80%** of the land wins outright.

A unique feature of this mode is the ability for players to form temporary [alliances](/Ally) with others during gameplay.

However, these [alliances](/Ally) are fragile: attacking an [allied](/Ally) player results in the attacker being marked as a [traitor](/Traitor) for a duration of 30 seconds.

While under this penalty, the [traitor](/Traitor)'s defenses are temporarily weakened, leaving them vulnerable to other players.

## Overtime {#Overtime}

Public FFA games have an **overtime** rule to stop stalemates: after **30 minutes**, the share of land required to win drops by **2 percentage points every minute** (from the usual 80%), with no floor, so a stalled game always ends once the leader crosses the falling bar. The current requirement is shown in the HUD. This is separate from the [Doomsday Clock](/Doomsday_Clock) modifier.

## Rules {#Rules}

Teaming, collusion and coordinated diplomacy in solo FFA lobbies are forbidden and punishable — see [Community Rules](/Community_Rules). Alliances made inside the game are fine; pre-arranging support with friends before joining is not.
