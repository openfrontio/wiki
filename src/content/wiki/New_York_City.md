The New York City map has 5 peices of land (boroughs) Manhattan, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island, and Brooklyn. Bronx is cut off in this map, but it shows some other parts of Long Island and New Jersey. The most islandous are south of the Long Island. Governors Island is also shown in the middle.
