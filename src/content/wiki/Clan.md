A **Clan** is a group of players in [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) who display a common tag. It is set by putting a tag in your username (for example, “[TAG] Player”).

### Mechanics {#Mechanics}

Members of the same clan are automatically placed on the same [team](/Teams) in [team](/Teams)-based modes.

### Exception {#Exception}

In [Free for All (FFA)](/Free_for_All) mode, clan tags do not confer any team affiliation; all participants compete individually, regardless of clan membership.
