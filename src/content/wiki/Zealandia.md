**Zealandia** is an Oceania map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 36.0](/Update_36.0), credited in the release notes to **thebananamoon** and **RickD004** — the mostly submerged continent around New Zealand drawn as dry land, so the Campbell Plateau, Challenger Plateau and Lord Howe Rise become playable territory alongside New Zealand, New Caledonia and the east coast of Australia.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Zealandia_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="591" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="2048" data-file-height="1890"><figcaption>The Zealandia map.</figcaption></figure>

Compared with the real-world [New Zealand](/New_Zealand) map there is far more land and much less open water, so games play more like a continental map, with the remaining sea channels as the main [warship](/Warship) and [trade](/Trade_Ship) routes.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 27 named [nations](/Nations), including Tasmania, Canberra, Brisbane, Great Barrier Reef, Campbell Plateau, New Caledonia, Challenger Plateau, Wellington, Lord Howe Rise, Titanosaurs, Bellona Island and Puysegur Islands.
