**Tribes** are the named bot factions that fill out public games (see [Bots](/Bots)). [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0) added **custom tribe names**, letting players buy a tribe name that can appear on bot tribes in public games.

## Custom tribe names {#Custom_tribe_names}

- Custom tribe names are bought and managed from the new **Tribes** tab in the store.
- A purchased tribe name can appear on [bot](/Bots) tribes in public games.
- **Boosts**, also bought in the store, make your tribe appear more often; the store shows when your next boost expires.
- A public tribe-name leaderboard tracks how tribes are doing, including their active boost counts.

## Tribe name themes {#Tribe_name_themes}

Update 33.0 also added a tribe name themes system for [maps](/Maps). Real-world maps ship with themed tribe names, and mappers can define custom tribes — including custom tribe spawn coordinates — in a map's `info.json`.

## Territory colour {#Territory_colour}

Since [Update 34.0](/Update_34.0), tribes are visually distinct from [nations](/Nations) on the map: tribes share one near-neutral grey colour family, while nations keep their muted palette. In [Free for All](/Free_for_All), tribes get the same flat colour they have in team games.
